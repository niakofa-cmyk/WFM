# 1. THE PRIMARY MISSION

We are evolving War for Mars into a fully integrated, immersive, competitive, multiplayer, infinite open-universe space combat game.

The newly established **Orbital Combat Lab is to become the foundation of the primary War for Mars game engine.**

We have previously developed multiple game builds, simulations, prototypes, operational environments, and open-world experiences. These contain valuable features, mechanics, technology, and gameplay systems that must not be abandoned simply because we have established a newer engine.

Your mission is to investigate those builds, understand what each one accomplished, identify the strongest and most useful capabilities, and systematically extract and integrate those capabilities into Orbital Combat Lab.

We are not building another independent Three.js universe.

We are not creating another disconnected flight simulator.

We are not simply combining visual assets.

We are consolidating the actual gameplay capabilities and underlying systems into the primary game engine.

**The objective is ONE MAIN ENGINE, ONE CONNECTED GAME, ONE EXPANDING UNIVERSE, AND MULTIPLE CONNECTED GAMEPLAY EXPERIENCES.**

The result must be substantially more advanced than any individual previous build.
