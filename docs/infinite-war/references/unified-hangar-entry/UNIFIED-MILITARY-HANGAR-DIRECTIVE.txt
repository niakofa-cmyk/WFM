# WFM — UNIFIED MILITARY HANGAR & SINGLE-ENTRY INFINITE UNIVERSE REDESIGN

## Mission

Reimagine and architecturally redesign WAR FOR MARS around one unified military hangar and one exclusive entry point into the Infinite Extended Universe.

This is not a cosmetic menu redesign. It is a complete restructuring of the player's game-entry experience, interface hierarchy, navigation architecture and integration of existing game systems.

## 1. Establish one unified player experience

Replace the fragmented entry experience with:

BOOT → PLAYER AUTHENTICATION → UNIFIED MILITARY HANGAR → ENTER THE UNIVERSE.

The military hangar must become the persistent home environment and central command center.

Do not introduce competing primary game-entry screens or separate home menus for campaign, arcade, multiplayer or exploration.

## 2. Build the military hangar

Create a visually impressive, fully integrated 3D military installation.

Include:
- The selected player spacecraft displayed prominently.
- A visible pilot and interactive ship inspection.
- Additional fleet vessels and military infrastructure.
- Hangar mechanics, maintenance systems and environmental activity.
- Live faction intelligence and war status.
- Fleet selection and shipyard.
- Weapon and equipment loadouts.
- Campaign and operational briefing systems.
- Multiplayer wing management.
- Technology and research.
- Trade, resources and station services.
- Commander profile and settings.

## 3. Establish a vertical-first interface

Design the interface for portrait mobile devices using a clear vertical hierarchy.

Provide a responsive landscape layout for flight and combat.

Use cinematic environmental presentation, refined military typography, layered HUD elements and restrained faction-specific lighting.

The ship and hangar must remain the dominant visual experience.

## 4. Establish one exclusive universe gateway

Create one clearly identifiable ENTER THE UNIVERSE action.

This is the exclusive primary gateway from the hangar into the Infinite Extended Universe.

Do not create independent entry buttons for separate universe destinations.

Once inside, the player navigates the actual universe to discover and access its locations, operations, stations, battles, fleets and exploration systems.

## 5. Integrate existing systems

Audit and preserve the existing:
- Campaign and narrative progression.
- Ship definitions and faction identity.
- Combat simulation.
- Multiplayer 5v5.
- Persistent player profiles.
- Server-validated progression and rewards.
- Travel and station services.
- Economy and technology systems.

Integrate them into the unified architecture rather than rebuilding functional systems unnecessarily.

## 6. Respect the master engine architecture

Use the established Orbital Engine as the authoritative rendering, simulation and world-integration architecture.

Do not introduce a parallel rendering engine or duplicate simulation.

Audit the current repository and resolve any discrepancy between the documented Three.js combat implementation and the intended Orbital Engine architecture before proceeding.

## 7. Implementation and verification

First perform a complete source audit.

Produce the target architecture, component map, navigation state model, data dependencies and migration plan.

Then implement in small, testable stages.

Validate:
- Existing functionality and regression safety.
- Real ship and environment rendering.
- Portrait and landscape responsiveness.
- Persistent player and world state.
- Actual multiplayer integration.
- Performance on physical mobile devices.
- Deployment and production behavior.

Do not claim a feature is implemented until its real integration and functionality have been demonstrated.

## Final design principle

ONE GAME. ONE HANGAR. ONE UNIVERSE. ONE ENTRY POINT.

The player should feel that they are entering a living military universe, not opening a collection of disconnected game menus.
