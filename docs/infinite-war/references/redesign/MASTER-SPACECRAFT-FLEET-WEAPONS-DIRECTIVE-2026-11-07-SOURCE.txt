# 7. DEVELOP AN ADVANCED MISSILE SYSTEM

This is a major priority.

Create distinct missile families with actual guidance and combat behavior.

## Missile types

### Heat-seeking missile

Tracks an eligible heat signature.

### Active sensor missile

Uses its own sensor guidance after launch.

### Semi-active missile

Requires continued targeting or illumination.

### Dumbfire rocket

No target lock. Depends on pilot accuracy.

### Heavy torpedo

Slower, more powerful, intended for capital ships.

### EMP missile

Disrupts selected electronic systems and sensors.

### Swarm missile

Splits into multiple bounded submunitions.

### Interceptor missile

Designed to engage incoming missiles or hostile drones.

### Stealth missile

Has reduced detection characteristics but appropriate balance limitations.

Each missile must have:

- Launch acceleration.
- Maximum velocity.
- Turn rate.
- Guidance authority.
- Fuel or lifetime.
- Lock requirements.
- Arming distance.
- Detonation radius.
- Damage profile.
- Target compatibility.
- Countermeasure susceptibility.
- Collision rules.
- Visual trail.
- Explosion behavior.

Implement target tracking using actual spatial positions and movement.

Do not allow instant-turning missiles or impossible homing.

Missile behavior must be deterministic and authoritative in competitive environments.
