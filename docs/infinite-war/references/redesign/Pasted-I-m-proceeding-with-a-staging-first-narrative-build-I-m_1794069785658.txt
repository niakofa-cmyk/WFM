### The resulting campaign structure

1. The Making of Eon Dusk — his wealth, scandals, political influence, and ambition.
2. The Quiet Mandate — the powerful inner circle that knows about the secret takeover.
3. The Unlisted Expedition — Dusk assembles his special force and launches for Mars.
4. Mars Is Not Empty — the expedition encounters the established Accord.
5. The Last Fleet — the expedition is destroyed, Dusk survives, and the emergency transmission escapes.
6. The Prisoner and Earth's Response — Dusk's custody, public disclosure, classified mobilization, and the signal mystery.
7. The First Martian Campaign — Cage, Iron Eclipse, and the expanding USSF–Accord conflict.
8. The First Witness — the buried Martian discovery and the opening toward the wider universe.

### Three important continuity improvements

Cage: Dusk is already an Accord prisoner before the mission begins. The transport is a later transfer. The new content defines three possible outcomes—USSF secures the transport, Accord completes the transfer, or the transport escapes in a contested state—without rewriting the historical capture.

Earth's response: The narrative distinguishes what the public learns, what a limited classified inner circle knows, and what remains unknown. This prevents the story from treating the entire United States government as a single conspiratorial organization.

First Witness: The evidence chain now connects the expedition flight recorder, emergency transmission, Pale Beacons, WARDEN-0's erasure order, Cage's custody manifest, and the buried Martian archive.
