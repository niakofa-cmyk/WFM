# 3. IMPLEMENT THE 360-DEGREE CIRCULAR GESTURE

## Clockwise gesture

A recognized clockwise circular movement triggers:

RIGHT BARREL ROLL.

## Counterclockwise gesture

A recognized counterclockwise circular movement triggers:

LEFT BARREL ROLL.

## Recognition requirements

Create a dedicated circular gesture recognizer.

It must evaluate:

- Pointer trajectory.
- Angular progression.
- Total angular sweep.
- Circle direction.
- Gesture duration.
- Gesture radius.
- Distance from the intended gesture center.
- Movement smoothness.
- Minimum gesture size.
- Maximum permitted gesture duration.
- Touch interruption.
- Gesture completion.
- Input cooldown.

Initially evaluate a full circular sweep of approximately 300–360 degrees, with configurable tolerance.

Do not require an unrealistically perfect geometric circle.

Use accumulated angular movement and radial consistency rather than a simplistic test that only checks whether the finger returned near its starting point.

The recognition system should:

1. Detect an intentional gesture candidate.
2. Track pointer movement.
3. Calculate angular change around the control's defined center.
4. Determine clockwise or counterclockwise direction.
5. Reject incomplete or accidental gestures.
6. Recognize a valid completed rotation.
7. Emit one maneuver command.
8. Prevent repeated activation from the same pointer movement.
9. Reset correctly on release, cancellation, interruption and timeout.

A recognized circular gesture should not generate uncontrolled aim movement while being evaluated.

Design a reliable input-arbitration strategy. Consider a short gesture-capture phase or a clearly discoverable hold-and-circle interaction, while leaving ordinary aiming immediately responsive.

The player should receive subtle visual feedback when a maneuver gesture is recognized.

Provide a directional roll indicator and a short maneuver confirmation effect.

- Spacecraft orientation.
- Angular velocity.
- Rotational acceleration.
- Flight camera.
- Ship model orientation.
- Engine and maneuver effects.
- Flight trajectory where applicable.
- Combat interaction.

Evaluate the distinction between:

- Pure axial roll.
- A controlled barrel-roll maneuver.
- A rolling evasive turn.

A pure axial roll rotates the spacecraft around its longitudinal axis.

A tactical barrel roll should combine controlled axial rotation with a calculated lateral or vertical trajectory change.

The spacecraft should complete a controlled maneuver rather than instantly snapping to a new orientation.

The maneuver should have a defined beginning, execution phase, completion, and recovery.

Allow the player to interrupt a maneuver with normal pilot input where safe and technically appropriate.
