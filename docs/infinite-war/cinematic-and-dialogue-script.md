# Infinite War — Cinematic and dialogue script v1.0

**Status:** Narrative script draft for the staging implementation.  
**Production note:** All historical scenes are archival reconstructions, recovered recordings, or present-day briefings. Do not present a reconstructed scene as a live event.

## Prologue — The Man Who Bought Tomorrow

**Presentation:** Archival cinematic montage at Phobos Yard evidence archive.  
**Trigger:** Player opens the first story archive after arriving at Phobos Yard.  
**Purpose:** Establish Dusk's wealth, influence, fear, and ambition without reducing the entire United States to a single conspiracy.

**Narrator (archival voiceover):**  
"Some men inherit power. Some build it. Eon Dusk learned how to purchase the doors, the people who guarded them, and the silence that followed."

**Visual sequence:**
- Public launch celebration; Dusk stands beside a new aerospace facility.
- A procurement hearing where a contract is challenged and the record is sealed.
- Robotics and AI manufacturing lines operating through the night.
- A private meeting where a defense supplier signs a classified addendum.
- Dusk alone, watching Earth from an orbital window.

**Dusk (private recording):**  
"Every report says we have time. Every committee says the next generation will solve it. I have read the models. I have seen what happens when a civilization mistakes delay for safety."

**Dusk (private recording):**  
"Mars is not a symbol. It is a decision. Whoever reaches it first decides what humanity becomes."

**Archivist:**  
"He called it continuity. The people he paid called it opportunity. The people he displaced called it something else."

## Scene 2 — The Quiet Mandate

**Presentation:** Briefing / document reconstruction.  
**Trigger:** The player verifies the sealed procurement chain.

**Mara Venn:**  
"This authorization carries government fingerprints, but it is not a government decision."

**Nadiya Sen:**  
"Some signatures are approval. Some are access. Some are people trying to keep a record in case this goes wrong. Do not turn a list of names into a verdict."

**Mara Venn:**  
"Who knew he intended to take Mars?"

**Nadiya Sen:**  
"Enough people to make the launch possible. Not enough to say the country chose it."

**Optional recovered memo — unnamed executive:**  
"Dusk believes he can make the first claim irreversible. We should not confuse his confidence with our control over him."

## Scene 3 — Departure Window

**Presentation:** Archival simulation.  
**Trigger:** Historical launch reconstruction reaches launch confirmation.

**Dusk:**  
"Earth has spent generations arguing over who deserves a future. Mars will not wait for another committee."

**Expedition AI:**  
"Command authority verified. Destination: Mars. Public mission designation: unavailable."

**Expedition Pilot:**  
"Sir, the civilian manifest does not match the force roster."

**Dusk:**  
"The manifest is what Earth is allowed to see. The mission is what we are going to do."

**Expedition Pilot:**  
"And if Mars is already inhabited?"

**Dusk:**  
"Then they will learn that we have arrived."

## Scene 4 — Mars Is Not Empty

**Presentation:** In-mission first-contact sequence.  
**Trigger:** Accord signatures resolve in the Mars Approach sector.

**Expedition Pilot:**  
"Command, those are not automated satellites. They are holding formation. Multiple vessels. Organized traffic."

**Dusk:**  
"Open the channel. Tell them this expedition is establishing a security perimeter."

**Accord Herald:**  
"You have entered a living world and named it empty. Withdraw your weapons and your claim."

**Dusk:**  
"We crossed the void to secure humanity's future."

**Accord Herald:**  
"Then why does your first act require our surrender?"

**Dusk:**  
"We did not cross the void to ask permission."

**Combat transition:** Accord defensive formations activate. The camera returns to the player's flight view before weapons release. No cutscene should remove player control for an extended combat segment.

## Scene 5 — The Last Fleet

**Presentation:** Recovered flight-recorder fragments at Rust Disc.  
**Trigger:** Player salvages the expedition flight recorder.

**Expedition Pilot (damaged recording):**  
"Expedition command, multiple hulls lost. We have no stable formation. The Accord has closed the return vector."

**Special Force Lead:**  
"All remaining units, converge on Dusk's carrier. Repeat, converge—"

**Expedition AI:**  
"Command ship integrity critical. Autonomous recovery unavailable."

**Unknown Crew Member:**  
"He's alive. Dusk is alive. They have him—"

**Transmission operator:**  
"Emergency packet going out. Earth Command, this is Expeditionary Unit Seven. Fleet destroyed. Eon Dusk alive. Accord custody confirmed. We are transmitting the last known coordinates—"

**Signal interference:**  
A second pattern interrupts the emergency packet. The audio is not translated or explained.

**Nadiya Sen (present day):**  
"The emergency message is human. The pattern underneath it is not part of the packet format. It was already there."

## Scene 6 — Earth's First Response

**Presentation:** Multi-location cinematic montage, then command briefing.  
**Trigger:** Server verifies the emergency packet evidence.

**Public broadcast anchor:**  
"An expedition connected to industrialist Eon Dusk has been destroyed near Mars. Unconfirmed reports indicate Dusk is alive and being held by an unidentified force. Federal officials have declined to answer questions about the expedition's authorization."

**Classified command channel — senior official:**  
"Do not describe this as a national invasion. We did not authorize the mission as it was executed."

**Corporate executive:**  
"That distinction will not recover the fleet. We have assets, contracts, and personnel on that planet."

**Military official:**  
"Then tell us who approved the command chain."

**Corporate executive:**  
"You know the names. You are asking who will say them aloud."

**Mara Venn (present day):**  
"We are being ordered to recover a prisoner, protect our people, and secure a theater built on an operation half the command structure never saw."

**Nadiya Sen:**  
"And the packet contains something the command structure still cannot explain."

## Scene 7 — Dusk in Custody

**Presentation:** Present-day Accord custody-room recording; Dusk is visible but not yet directly playable.  
**Trigger:** Player recovers the prisoner manifest and reaches the Cage debrief.

**Tarek Sol:**  
"You came to claim a world. Now you ask why we hold you."

**Dusk:**  
"You hold one man. Earth will send fleets."

**Tarek Sol:**  
"Your fleets came before your people understood why. Do not mistake the response for the reason."

**Dusk:**  
"You think you can keep Mars forever?"

**Tarek Sol:**  
"We think no one has the right to take it by surprise and call that destiny."

**Dusk:**  
"Then you will learn what Earth is willing to spend."

**Tarek Sol:**  
"We have already seen what you were willing to spend."

## Scene 8 — Cage: The Prisoner Transfer

**Presentation:** Two faction-specific mission briefings and one shared debrief.  
**Trigger:** Cage operation begins.

### USSF briefing

**Mara Venn:**  
"Dusk is a prisoner. The Accord is moving him under guard. Your orders are to intercept the transport, verify custody, and avoid destroying the prisoner module."

**Pilot:**  
"And if the escort refuses?"

**Mara Venn:**  
"Disable the escort. We need a living witness, not a wreck with a flag on it."

### Accord briefing

**Tarek Sol:**  
"The prisoner is being transferred under Accord custody. The incoming USSF wing will call this a rescue. Do not let them turn custody into a battlefield execution."

**Accord Pilot:**  
"And Dusk?"

**Tarek Sol:**  
"He will answer for what he attempted. We do not become what we oppose."

### Shared evidence debrief

**Nadiya Sen:**  
"The custody manifest is authentic. Dusk survived the destruction of the expedition. The transfer recorder carries the same pattern as the Pale Beacons."

**Mara Venn:**  
"Then the war began over a man. The thing beneath it began long before he arrived."

## Scene 9 — Iron Eclipse

**Presentation:** Fleet-operation cinematic.  
**Trigger:** Cage reaches a verified terminal outcome and Iron Eclipse is unlocked.

**Mara Venn:**  
"This is no longer a rescue corridor. Both fleets are committing capital assets. Keep your wing together and make every shot count."

**Tarek Sol:**  
"A world is not secured by occupying its sky. Hold the line, protect the living, and do not mistake victory for justice."

**Wing AI:**  
"Capital target identified. Point-defense grid active. Shield array stable. Reactor signature exposed."

**Pilot command prompt:**  
"Choose your order: attack, defend, escort, or regroup."

The cinematic ends before tactical control begins. Tactical orders remain gameplay, not a pre-rendered sequence.

## Scene 10 — The First Witness

**Presentation:** Subsurface discovery cinematic, only after the Orbital-native environment and interaction are implemented.  
**Trigger:** Player verifies the buried archive evidence.

**Nadiya Sen:**  
"This is not a broadcast tower. It is not speaking to us in any language we know."

**Tarek Sol:**  
"The oldest record is not always a command. Sometimes it is a warning."

**Mara Venn:**  
"Can we know who made it?"

**Nadiya Sen:**  
"Not yet. We can know what it is not. It is not a message sent by Dusk. It is not a weapon built for this war. And it was here before any of us."

**Unknown Signal:**  
"A world remembers what power tries to erase."

**End card:**  
"THE FIRST WITNESS — THE MARTIAN RECORD IS ONLY THE BEGINNING."

## Performance and accessibility notes

- Keep critical flight and combat audio above nonessential dialogue.
- Provide subtitles, speaker identity, transcript replay, and adjustable dialogue volume.
- Allow cinematics to be skipped without skipping the evidence or progression event.
- Avoid long forced camera locks during combat.
- Use short in-flight radio exchanges and reserve longer exposition for briefings, debriefs, and archive playback.
- Each line must be localized through stable scene and line keys before release.
- Any faction-specific line must be selected by server-authorized faction context.
