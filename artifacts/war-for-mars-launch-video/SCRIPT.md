# WAR FOR MARS — 5v5 Launch Film

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** A live wing operation on Mars feels immediate, coordinated, and playable alone or together.
- **Identity:** The supplied WAR FOR MARS mark and Mars landscape; soot-black, iron, bone, solar amber, and Accord red. Display typography in Barlow Condensed; Figtree for system copy.
- **Hero:** Ten starfighters converging over the rust frontier, framed as a live tactical match rather than a generic space battle.
- **Motion:** Radar aperture-to-world reveal, hard graphic match cuts on the reticle, and tightly choreographed split-team formations.
- **Arc:** The barren horizon becomes a tactical room, the room ignites into a live fight, then the battle resolves into the game's insignia.
- **Payoff:** A still, monumental insignia replaces the busy battle, while its diagonal mark becomes the opening radar sweep for a seamless loop.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape, 24 seconds.

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.138s / 4.138s — “CONTACT”
**Purpose:** Hook with a planetary scale and communicate that the conflict is team-sized.

**Exact copy:** `MARS / FRONTIER` appears at 0.20s and holds to 3.72s, top-left. `10 CONTACTS` snaps in at 1.12s, lower-left, holds to 3.72s. `5 VS 5` appears at 2.20s, lower-right, holds to 3.72s. No narration.

**Composition and artistic idea:** The supplied Mars landscape fills the 16:9 frame, horizon at 57% height. A narrow amber radar line slices the image at frame 0, an impossible tactical instrument cutting through a real landscape. The scene starts on a macro crop of the orange horizon and pulls out to reveal ten tiny-but-clear lights arranged in opposing arcs. Scanline ticks and technical labels occupy only the top-left and lower-left; the horizon and ship contacts own the center.

**Choreography:** 0.00–0.38s radar slit brightens and expands into a circular amber reticle (the second graphic element is visible from frame 0). 0.38–1.05s a camera-scale pullout reveals Mars and a distant craft silhouette. 0.60–1.45s five amber contacts pop one every 0.16s; 1.45–2.15s five red contacts answer from the opposite arc. `10 CONTACTS` snaps at 1.12s with a short blur-slice; `5 VS 5` stamps at 2.20s. 2.60–3.30s the contact arcs rotate toward one another, the landscape subtly parallax-shifts. At 3.62s the ring accelerates forward toward lens center, carrying the cut on the 4.138s onset; no still tail.

**Transition contract:** The outgoing amber reticle is 1.5x and centered at 4.138s; the incoming tactical shot opens on the same amber circle at 1.5x, 10px blur. The circle sharpens while a horizontal team rail unfolds behind it.

**Assets and technique:** Existing `public/images/mars-front.jpg`; SVG/CSS contacts, reticle, grain, Barlow Condensed typography. No fabricated gameplay screenshot.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental only. Reticle opens on the first low transient; the two contact waves ride successive beat accents. Planned accents are unverified until music generation and onset check.
