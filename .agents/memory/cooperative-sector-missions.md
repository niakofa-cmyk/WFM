Cooperative play is native to the continuous shared Orbital universe: nearby
allied pilots should fly into the same live fleet encounter and help without
launching a separate co-op world. Grouping and shared objectives may remain;
they must not create a disconnected default player journey.

**Why:** the owner explicitly clarified that multiple users share the universe,
encounter fleets and assist each other in live battles.

**How to apply:** extend the existing authoritative encounter and contribution
contract rather than reserve a duplicate fleet or trust a client wallet.
Attendance alone must not fabricate kills. Revalidate admission for late allies.
Keep Rust Disc → Mars Approach → Pale Beacons's legacy sector runtime and saved
discoveries as compatibility/rollback until continuous-world objectives are
verified. Do not replace Wing or alter original campaign clears. Production
remains gated; local tests and staging evidence are not production approval.
