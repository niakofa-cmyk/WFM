description: Owner requires one advancing multiplayer story and one finale, not per-player campaign runtimes.

The owner requires a single shared story timeline. New users join the current
world completion; leaving at 30% and returning at 70% means the world advanced
through other pilots' actions. Absence must not pause or reset the campaign.
At 100%, that campaign ends for everyone with celebrations and permanently
recorded rewards, XP and equipment. Do not automatically start a private replay
or a new season for newcomers after completion.

**Why:** the owner explicitly rejected multiple runtime experiences and
per-account campaign timelines. Shared spatial regions are parts of one
campaign, not separate stories.

**How to apply:** derive live chapter availability and campaign completion from
shared server state, while preserving account-scoped inventories, awards,
knowledge and historical choices. Authored history must not be destroyed during
migration. Keep diagnostic training outside normal player entry and never count
its fake or replay results toward the shared campaign. Environment isolation
still applies: Development, staging and Production must not share databases.

The uploaded 50,000-operation target, final rewards, 450-client capacity and
sub-2ms latency are proposals, not owner-certified balance or measured evidence.
Do not equate a counter implementation with completion of the authored storyline.
