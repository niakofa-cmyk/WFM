---
name: Six military encounter design principles
description: Creator requirements for physical equipment, tactical enemies and environments, orchestration, learning and aesthetics
---

Apply six principles to WFM's one living Orbital universe:

1. Weapons are enemy-carried, physically recoverable military hardware that
   mounts compatibly on ships, not only menu choices.
2. Enemy components have distinct tactical purposes and consequences.
3. Environments participate in combat rather than merely decorating it.
4. Formation behavior, placement, environmental timing and phases compose
   memorable encounters without removing free three-dimensional movement.
5. Understanding movement, weapons, enemies, environment and objectives
   improves a pilot's outcomes.
6. Ships, equipment, environments and audio share one industrial military
   science-fiction aesthetic.

**Why:** The creator explicitly requested all six as the design foundation,
not a literal recreation of a side-scrolling game's stages.

**How to apply:** Use the acceptance distinctions in
`docs/infinite-war/EINHANDER-ORBITAL-DESIGN-TRANSLATION.md`. Do not equate a
catalog-selected drop with provenance from an enemy's actual fitted module,
collision with destructibility, or automated fixtures with two-phone evidence.
Preserve authoritative shared state, licensed/original assets and player camera
control when extending these systems.
