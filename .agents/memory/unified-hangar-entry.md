The WFM player enters one persistent landscape-first command hangar, sees their
saved hull and faction, can choose USSF or Accord there, then uses one dominant
**ENTER THE UNIVERSE** gateway. Destinations belong inside the connected
theater, not in competing launch buttons on the hangar. Fleet, operations, Wing,
profile, and settings remain accessible as secondary command systems. Hangar
orientation is landscape-only; portrait shows a rotate-device screen rather
than a vertical reflow.

**Why:** the owner wants a unified military home and a single entry into the
Infinite Universe. The owner's latest clarification supersedes earlier
portrait-first source wording: the operational hangar is landscape/full-screen.

**How to apply:** make explicit launch into the main Orbital flight scene the primary gateway;
persist faction selection through the existing loadout service. Do not expose
separate solo, offline, training, or expedition launch paths. The one approved
alternate gameplay is Arcade Skirmish 5v5 competition entered at the multiplayer
asteroid from within the live universe, never directly from the hangar.
Legacy gameplay URLs return to the hangar; only its explicit gateway launches flight.
Three.js remains the primary
universe renderer, with native WebGL2 a companion; neither owns separate
gameplay authority. Historical renderer-removal directions are superseded.

**Why:** the owner repeatedly clarified that all playable activity must use the
one live Orbital universe entered through the hangar, with only the asteroid
competition exception explicitly clarified in their latest request.

The explicit hangar launch should open with a Combat Lab-style live patrol
battle, then let the pilot continue exploring the same universe. This is a
present-day encounter, not a forced historical Dusk defeat or another training
destination. A reconnect must preserve the existing craft, not replay an
opening or repair damage.

**Why:** the owner explicitly requested the training-style initial air battle
as the game's entry encounter and asked to save that direction.

**How to apply:** integrate training behavior through the shared server's
existing patrol/flight/settlement authority; do not mint rewardable training
enemies or replace hangar-first explicit entry.
