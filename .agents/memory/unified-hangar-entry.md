description: Owner-directed WFM opening experience and single-gateway principle.
---

The WFM player enters one persistent landscape-first command hangar, sees their
saved hull and faction, can choose USSF or Accord there, then uses one dominant
**ENTER THE UNIVERSE** gateway. Destinations belong inside the connected
theater, not in competing launch buttons on the hangar. Fleet, operations, Wing,
profile, and settings remain accessible as secondary command systems. Hangar
orientation is landscape-only; portrait shows a rotate-device screen rather
than a vertical reflow.

**Why:** the owner wants a unified military home and a single entry into the
Infinite Universe. The owner's latest clarification supersedes earlier
portrait-first source wording: the operational hangar is landscape/full-screen.

**How to apply:** make the connected account theater the primary gateway;
persist faction selection through the existing loadout service; retain offline
flight only as explicitly no-reward practice. Use Orbital-native rendering for
newly migrated surfaces, but do not claim the full Three.js migration complete
while legacy operation paths remain.
