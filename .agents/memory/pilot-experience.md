**Rule:** Keep the ship, flight space, and combat readable. In offline Orbital, left-stick up/down drives forward/reverse throttle and left/right turns with coordinated bank; the right stick retains pitch/aim. A deliberate right-stick circle around the visible stick center triggers one barrel roll while ordinary aim continues during the gesture; allow up to three seconds and do not require a hidden hold or mode switch. Execute the accepted roll through bounded quaternion rotation and lateral thrust in the simulation, with energy/cooldown limits and no invulnerability. Same-sector edge double-taps remain short vector-thrust pulses (up/down vertical, left/right lateral), and the up-arrow action is vertical thrust. Keep target lock and the maneuver drawer on the left; keep missiles and primary fire by right-stick aim. Do not show a duplicate forward button by default. Preserve Rust Disc, server-authoritative co-op, and live Wing mappings; do not send the local roll intent through their existing adapters.

**Why:** The user clarified on 2026-11-05 that the Orbital left stick must be the primary propulsion control: up/down drives forward/reverse, horizontal drag turns while thrusting, and the up-arrow action is vertical rather than forward thrust. They also want target lock grouped with left-side flight controls while missiles and primary fire stay right. On 2026-11-05 they added a right-thumb circular barrel-roll gesture as a tactical action, not a visual-only animation, and reported the initial hidden hold prerequisite did not trigger on their phone. Browser verification exposed that a touch-origin-centered recognizer did not match a loop around the visible stick; anchor the recognizer to the stick center and allow a deliberate loop of up to three seconds. Rust Disc's source currently uses left-stick yaw/pitch with a separate throttle action, so do not claim its behavior is identical. The landscape reference still defines the HUD grouping, and live Wing's validated mapping must remain intact.

**How to apply:** Extend the existing `PilotFlightStick → input → Orbital simulation` path, not a second loop. Keep left-stick Y on signed throttle, X on yaw/coordinated bank, and right-stick aim/pitch active while a continuous right-thumb circle is recognized. Keep the recognizer pure and test direction, sweep, radius, time, cancellation, and duplicate suppression. Keep the command offline-only until there is a separately versioned and server-validated network contract. Place lock and the maneuver drawer with the left stick; leave missiles and primary fire with right-stick aim. Maintain neutral touch origin, release/cancel reset, and deterministic six-axis physics. Do not treat browser emulation as physical-device certification; follow [the live Wing migration gates](../../docs/infinite-war/LIVE-WING-FLIGHT-CONTROL-MIGRATION-PLAN.md).
