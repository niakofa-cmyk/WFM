The full user-supplied tactical control documents and barrel-roll reference image are retained in `docs/infinite-war/references/`.

**Rule:** Keep the ship, flight space, and combat readable. In offline Orbital, left-stick up/down drives forward/reverse throttle and left/right turns with coordinated bank; the right stick retains pitch/aim. Keep both sticks in their established positions and sizes. Keep only target lock beside the left flight stick; place the maneuver drawer and other flight-action tabs on the right with the combat controls. A deliberate right-stick circle around the visible stick center triggers one barrel roll while ordinary aim continues during the gesture; allow up to three seconds and do not require a hidden hold or mode switch. Execute the accepted roll through bounded quaternion rotation and lateral thrust in the simulation, with energy/cooldown limits and no invulnerability. During the roll, stabilize the follow camera so it does not counter-rotate and hide the ship's full rotation; blend back smoothly afterward. Same-sector edge double-taps remain short vector-thrust pulses (up/down vertical, left/right lateral), and the up-arrow action is vertical thrust. Keep missiles and primary fire by right-stick aim. Do not show a duplicate forward button by default. Preserve Rust Disc, server-authoritative co-op, and live Wing mappings; do not send the local roll intent through their existing adapters.

**Why:** The user clarified on 2026-11-05 that the Orbital left stick must be the primary propulsion control: up/down drives forward/reverse, horizontal drag turns while thrusting, and the up-arrow action is vertical rather than forward thrust. They later specified that stick positions and sizes stay unchanged, only target lock remains as an action beside the left stick, and maneuver/flight-action tabs belong on the right. They also added a right-thumb circular barrel-roll gesture as a tactical action, not a visual-only animation, and reported the initial hidden hold prerequisite did not trigger on their phone. The camera previously followed the ship's roll orientation, which could make a full simulation rotation look incomplete from the pilot view. Browser verification exposed that a touch-origin-centered recognizer did not match a loop around the visible stick; anchor the recognizer to the stick center and allow a deliberate loop of up to three seconds. Rust Disc's source currently uses left-stick yaw/pitch with a separate throttle action, so do not claim its behavior is identical. The landscape reference still defines the HUD grouping, and live Wing's validated mapping must remain intact.

**How to apply:** Extend the existing `PilotFlightStick → input → Orbital simulation` path, not a second loop. Keep left-stick Y on signed throttle, X on yaw/coordinated bank, and right-stick aim/pitch active while a continuous right-thumb circle is recognized. Keep the recognizer pure and test direction, sweep, radius, time, cancellation, and duplicate suppression. Keep the command offline-only until there is a separately versioned and server-validated network contract. Keep the sticks' size and side unchanged; keep target lock beside the left flight stick; place the maneuver drawer and other flight-action tabs on the right near missiles and primary fire. Stabilize camera follow vectors during the roll and fade back after completion. Maintain neutral touch origin, release/cancel reset, and deterministic six-axis physics. Do not treat browser emulation as physical-device certification; follow [the live Wing migration gates](../../docs/infinite-war/LIVE-WING-FLIGHT-CONTROL-MIGRATION-PLAN.md).
