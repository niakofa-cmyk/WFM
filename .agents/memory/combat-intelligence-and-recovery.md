name: Combat intelligence and recovery direction
description: Owner's AI rear-positioning, evasion and automatic energy-recovery goals
---

Enemies should fight for useful rear-quarter firing positions and physically
evade incoming attacks, rather than act as stationary targets. Confirmed hostile
kills should automatically restore maneuver energy and support kill milestones
while pilots continue exploring the one shared universe.

**Why:** the owner explicitly requested this combat loop and supplied the
combat-intelligence/progression reference on 2026-11-11. Its reward percentages,
lifetime achievements and AI tiers are proposals, not proof of implementation.

**How to apply:** retain server-owned destruction, attribution and resource
limits. Prefer capped resource/recognition rewards over escalating permanent
damage advantages. Preserve existing currency settlement and separate
competitive Wing balance. Distinguish checkpoint-retained flight progress from
an account-lifetime achievement ledger or real deployed persistence.

Reward recovery must preserve both attribution and the damage/death state used
to reproduce a receipt; an intact starting roster alone is insufficient.

**Why:** a multi-pilot patrol-kill recovery check exposed a legitimate receipt
mismatch when restoration reconstructed the initial living roster.

**How to apply:** round-trip partially damaged and finalized encounters,
including late allies and NPC kills. Keep fingerprint validation strict instead
of treating a mismatch as permission to reissue or skip a reward.
