Build Phases 1–3 together: analog right-thumb hull movement, eight-direction
bursts, and advanced evasion/recovery in the existing Orbital simulation.
Preserve the classic aim option and the established two-thumb layout.

**Why:** The owner explicitly selected the combined first package rather than
isolated visual gestures. Evasion should move the ship physically while pilots
can still aim/fire, without granting invulnerability.

**How to apply:** Use
[the package and retained references](../../docs/infinite-war/PHASE-1-3-PILOT-MANEUVER-PACKAGE.md).
Keep hybrid as the compatible default. The optional vector-only profile exists
because a pilot may want to move out of a gun line without the right thumb also
turning the firing direction; other steering inputs remain independent.

Preserve one continuous universe and canonical simulation. Treat low-allocation
loops, lazy loading, bounded actor budgets, mobile-conscious geometry and
measured frame/network timing as performance goals—not guarantees of zero lag
or zero garbage collection. Complete and verify existing fleet capabilities
before adopting the uploaded expansion roadmap wholesale.

**Why:** The owner asked to retain the spacecraft, performance and maneuver
documents as foundational goals. The samples contain aspirational claims and
shortcuts that are not evidence of genuine combat or mobile readiness.

**How to apply:** Never substitute hard-coded pilots, direct enemy-health
changes, a second movement engine, or unnegotiated network intents for verified
flight. Keep local roll/drift and supported shared controls clearly distinct
until a guarded multiplayer upgrade is tested; preserve replay and release gates.
