---
name: Living enemy formations
description: Build persistent roaming patrol operations on top of the canonical Orbital universe.
---

Roaming fleet behavior belongs in the existing server-owned Orbital simulation:
stable hull identities, formations, command succession, bounded sensor memory,
casualty-driven retreat, and real existing-patrol assistance. Do not substitute
scripted one-stage spawns or fabricate reinforcement ships.

**Why:** the user wants a living military universe whose fleets patrol, detect,
coordinate, fight, and persist, without splitting gameplay into another runtime.

**How to apply:** preserve the existing roster, lease, reward, and checkpoint
contracts. Fleet reinforcement requests are not dispatched support; route only
real persistent assets and prove cross-instance ownership before claiming
global persistence.

Encounter presentation should follow the same evidence: sensor contact, approach,
active battle, human/AI assistance, command-ship damage, withdrawal, and settlement
are separate observable beats. Show ship roles on the actual 3D actors and retain
the battle conclusion after the last hostile contact disappears. Incoming threat
indicators must be tied to server-tracked projectiles. A second nearby formation
is an additional contact, not a claimed reinforcement; never fabricate a battle
beat that the authoritative runtime has not produced.

**Why:** the owner wants battles to read with a clear beginning, middle, and end
while pilots remain free to maneuver in the shared three-dimensional universe.

**How to apply:** derive cues from bounded nearby server snapshots and settled
encounter state. Keep the tactical readout compact and presentation-only; do not
change combat authority, fleet ownership, or reward settlement.
